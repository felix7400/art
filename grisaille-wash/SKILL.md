# Grisaille Wash

Grey watercolour on warm paper: one pigment in many dilutions, fog as paper left bare, small dark figures in frock coats and top hats seen from far away, and tree trunks between them and us.

## Rules
1. One pigment only. Every grey is the same neutral grey multiplied onto the paper at 6–95 % strength.
2. Every area is a wash, not a fill: a shape deformed and stacked 20–30 times at low opacity, glazed over what lies below.
3. Fog, distance and light are paper brought back over the paint. Far things are paler and higher; bottoms dissolve, tops stay crisp.
4. Vertical trunks give the rhythm, cropped at the top: pale ghosts far away, one dark opaque trunk in front of the action.
5. Figures are small (about 15 % of the height) and the darkest, sharpest thing in the picture. Seen from the side or from behind.
6. Light from one side; a figure casts a single thin dark stroke on the ground, away from the light.
7. Tell the story with posture, weather and distance. Something enormous happens quietly.

## Palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #ECE9E2 | bare paper, fog, light |
| pigment | #2B2D30 | every wash, multiplied at a strength |
| ink | #1C1D1F | full strength: coat, hat, darkest trunk |

## Texture
Pigment pools at the drying edge of each wash. Slow mottling inside, granulation in the paper tooth, a few back-runs with dark fractal rims and dried tide lines. Figures are painted too: dark edges, a little wet light inside, a hair of paper between near limbs and body.

## Never
- a second colour, pure white or pure black
- flat vector fills or smooth digital gradients
- glows, lens effects, close-ups, faces with expressions
- text, speech or captions in the picture
- violence shown on screen

## Code
